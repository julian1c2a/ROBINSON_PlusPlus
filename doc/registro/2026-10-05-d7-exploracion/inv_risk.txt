===== risk:kernel-eval 13109
**D7 kernel-evaluation risk (RPP 8fd14f4, FOL 782928c)**

I only read sources. I ran no Lean.

**Bottom line:** D7 does not make symbol codes newly evaluable by the kernel. They already are today. What D7 changes is the cost of evaluating them, the definition heights, the spelling of symbols and the equality instance. The real barrier to evaluation is unary `triN` and `numeralM`, and D7 does not touch either.

## 0. The premise does not hold for v4.31.0

**Evidence from the toolchain sources (INFERENCE):**
- `String.toList` is defined as `(String.Internal.toArray s).toList` (`Init/Data/String/Basic.lean:240-242`).
- `toArray` calls `ByteArray.utf8Decode?` (`:222-223`). Its inner loop `go` is marked `@[semireducible]` and uses `termination_by b.size - i` (`:74-86`).
- Well-founded definitions with a Nat measure are compiled to `WellFounded.Nat.fix` (`Lean/Elab/PreDefinition/WF/Fix.lean:281-301`; `Main.lean:62-67` warns about `@[semireducible]` on any other kind).
- `Nat.fix` is documented as "reduces on closed terms" (`Init/WF.lean:478-497`).
- The built-in `String.reduceToList` dsimproc depends on that reduction (`Lean/Meta/Tactic/Simp/BuiltinSimprocs/String.lean:39-42`).

**The build already relies on it:**
- `Meta/Hilbert.lean:69-74` (`axiomsBase_lift_eq`) proves its statement with `rfl` ("por cómputo"). It sets `maxRecDepth 20000`, and its docstring says this "cubre los numerales de símbolos (σ = 963)".
- The 141 base axioms contain closed codes: `strCodeM zero_sym` and `strCodeM succ_sym` (`Minimal/Axioms.lean:894-899`), and `termCodeM` of `zero`, `succ #0`, `nil` and `cons #1 #0` (`:1158-1179`, `:1543-1545`).
- `strCodeM s` is `charsCodeM s.toList` (`:589`). This `rfl` cannot be checked without decoding "0", "σ" and "::" and walking `numeralM` 48, 963 and 58 (INFERENCE from the definitions; the proof is in the green build).

**The barrier D7 leaves alone** (unary `triN`, `Minimal/Axioms.lean:626-632`; unary `numeralM`, `:571-573`). Values computed from the definitions (MEASURED):

| Code | Value |
|---|---|
| `codeNatStr "0"` | 1,177 |
| `codeNatStr "σ"` | 464,167 |
| `codeNatStr "::"` | 1,569,048 |
| `codeNatStr "∈"` | 37,953,829 |
| TC0 = `tcFnN 0` | 241,120,331,829 (needs `triN 694,434`) |
| TCS | ≈5.80·10²¹ (needs `triN ≈1.08·10¹¹`) |

So any Nat-level code with a symbol inside a `consN`, and therefore the anchor, `V₀`, `godelCN` and `liar`, is out of the kernel's reach both before and after D7.

**What D7 does change:**
- Definition heights: the `String.toList` and `String.decEq` layers disappear from every constant built on symbols.
- The spelling of symbols, and the compiled form of the `MNV` dispatch.
- The equality instance: `String.decEq` becomes `instDecidableEqList` on `Char`.

**A cheap check to settle this before D7** (not run). Put three examples in a sondeo:
- `"σ".toList = ['σ'] := by decide +kernel` (should pass today).
- `codeNatStr "σ" = 464167 := by decide +kernel` (should pass today).
- `set_option maxHeartbeats 2000 in #guard_msgs in example : tcFnN 0 = 241120331829 := by decide +kernel` (should fail in the kernel).

`decide +kernel` exists in v4.31 (`Init/Tactics.lean:1377-1381`), and the kernel honours `maxHeartbeats` (`Lean/AddDecl.lean:25,29`; `Lean/Environment.lean:274-277`).

## 1. Ranked list (likelihood D7 changes kernel behaviour × damage)

**R1 — MEDIUM-HIGH: closed `rfl` closes over Nat-level symbol codes (no free variables)**
- Where:
  - `Meta/ModeloCodificacion.lean:126-129` (`v_tc_zero`, `v_tc_succ`: `abre; rfl`).
  - `:189-194` (`v_vpf_ind`, `v_vpf_listInd`).
  - `:245` and `:283-288` (`lwf` on tags 18 and 20).
  - `:367-368`.
  - The data they use: `Meta/ModeloCodigo.lean:445-454`, `477-482`, `591`, `593`.
- Why it is risky: they pass only because both sides become syntactically equal after unfolding names (TC0 → `codeNatTerm zero`, `zero_sym` → literal). On closed terms the kernel's eager Nat evaluation is armed (INFERENCE from the kernel algorithm). If D7 makes one side differ, the kernel goes to `triN 694,434` and hits deep recursion or hangs. Examples of such drift: `tcFnN` switches to `codeNatChars` but `ev_strCodeM` still produces `codeNatStr`, or `abre` gains `consN`/`codeNatChars` so one side turns into arithmetic.
- Mitigation:
  - Unfold explicitly with `rw [tcFnN, TC0, TCS, TNIL, TCONS]`, then close with `with_reducible rfl`, which fails fast (`rw` itself only tries this; `Init/Tactics.lean:609`).
  - Keep the `*_sym` constants folded: remove them from `abre` (`Meta/ModeloEstandar.lean:238-246`) and state the `MN_*` lemmas over the constants.
  - Rewrite with a ∀-lemma such as `evalTerm … (cons (numeralM 1) (cons (strCodeM s) (cons nil nil))) = consN 1 (consN (codeNatChars s) (consN 0 0))`.

**R2 — MEDIUM: existing proofs that do evaluate symbol codes at term level**
- (a) `Meta/Hilbert.lean:69-74`, described in §0.
- (b) `Meta/ReprPrf.lean:871`, `893`, `1055`, `1084` (`prf_lineWF_ind`, `prf_lineWF_listInd` and their `_imp` forms). Their `simp only` sets (for example `:876-877`) do not include `substTerm_termCodeM` (`Minimal/Axioms.lean:750`). So `substTerm 0 X (termCodeM …)` is closed by evaluation in the final `exact` (INFERENCE). The file also sets `maxRecDepth 16000` (`:30`). For contrast, `HasWitTcFnPrf.lean:220-224` does include `substTerm_strCodeM`.
- Why it is risky: the cost is bounded today, but these proofs are the pattern for "compute through codes". Spelling symbols as `"…".toList` adds a decode to every comparison, a non-ASCII symbol (∈ = 8,712) multiplies the depth, and extending (a) to `axioms` would reach the anchor.
- Mitigation: use the closure lemmas instead (`Minimal/Axioms.lean:684-724`, `745-750`), then lower the raised limits. MEASURED: the build has 24 `set_option maxRecDepth` lines (2×40000, 3×20000, 1×16000, 17×8000, 1×4000).

**R3 — MEDIUM: the D7 epicentre, the `MNV` model dispatch**
- Where (MEASURED): `Meta/ModeloEstandar.lean:48-87` (33 String patterns), `:91-115` (25 `MN_*` `rfl` lemmas, with `V` a variable), `:238-246` (`abre`), `:250-454` (34 `v_*` proofs using `simp [..., MNV]`). 33 + 25 = 58, which matches ADR-122's figure for the dispatch.
- Why it is risky: the matcher gets recompiled from a `String.decEq` chain into a List/Char decision tree. If the spelling drifts, simp lemmas stop firing. A `"σ".toList` in a kernel-checked position costs a UTF-8 decode, whereas today's `String.decEq` only encodes (`Init/Prelude.lean:3534`).
- Mitigation: use one spelling only, `def succ_sym : List Char := ['σ']`, with patterns identical to the constants' bodies, and never `.toList` of a literal. Measure compile time and memory against ADR-120 (17 s and 38 s, 5.1 GB; quoted, not re-measured).

**R4 — MEDIUM-LOW (unlikely, but catastrophic): the only places `V₀` meets a defeq check**
- Where: `Meta/ModeloCodificacion.lean:325-335` (`V₀_def` by `rfl`; `v_ancla` ending in `exact V₀_def`), `Meta/ModeloCodigo.lean:559-561`, `Meta/Consistencia.lean:43-47` and `:62-70`.
- Why it is risky: if D7 edits the body of `V₀` but not the hand-written right-hand side of `V₀_def`, the check becomes a real closed defeq and hits the measured trap.
- Mitigation: use the generated equation lemma (`V₀.eq_1`, or `rw [V₀]`), and end `v_ancla` with `rw` instead of `exact`.

**R5 — MEDIUM-LOW: closed astronomical numeral under `simpa`**
- Where: `Meta/GodelTwoPrf.lean:146-150` (`rw … at hiff; simpa only [godelCN, selfAppN, provCodeN, …]`), with the definitions in `Meta/DiagonalNumeral.lean:60-67`.
- Why it is risky: `simpa` ends with a default-transparency defeq. If heights or normal forms change, the check may unfold towards `codeNat godelBeta'`.
- Mitigation: an explicit `rw` chain closed with `with_reducible`, or prove the statement for an arbitrary numeral `N` and instantiate.

**R6 — LOW-MEDIUM: kernel rejection rather than evaluation**
- Where: `Meta/CodeDecode.lean:25-26`, `54-65`, `167`, `211-218`, and the memory note `feedback_lean_kernel_ite_string`.
- D7 swaps the `ite` instance to `instDecidableEqList`. Keep the functional-induction workaround and re-test `decodeTerm_inj` and `decodeForm_inj` first.

**R7 — LOW: the anchor family**

- These are generic in `L` and `ψ`, and the anchor enters only by application, so D7 leaves them unchanged.
- Keep `subst_psiD` (`:1103-1107`) free of `"…".toList` literals; the dsimproc would add kernel-checked decode steps.

**R8 — LOW: list-spine computations only**

**R9 — LOW (gets cheaper with D7): symbol inequality by `decide`**
- Where: `Meta/Godel.lean:85-92` and `122-126`; `Meta/Provability.lean:68-72`; `FOL/FOL/DecEq.lean:49-50`.

**R10 — LOW: docstrings that give the wrong reason**

- `Meta/ModeloEstandar.lean:16-18` and `Meta/Consistencia.lean:39-41` explain the `V₀` trap "by height". See §3.

## 3. The two traps

**(a) The definition-height trap** (recorded at `DECISIONS.md:9277-9280`: 2.5 GB in 12 s; quoted, not re-measured)
- D7 lowers the heights above the symbol layer. The order stays the same between a definition and what it is built from (`V₀` over `concatN`; TC0 over `codeNatTerm`; the anchor over `axD` over `nD`). Between unrelated constants (`MNV` and `V₀`) the order may change; I have not measured this.
- The measured trap does not depend on that order: the left side is stuck at a projection of `MNV V₀`, so `V₀` is the only side the kernel can unfold (INFERENCE). D7 neither fixes it nor creates it.
- Keep proving for a variable `V` and instantiating. Add the change in R4 and the canary. `opaque` is not an option: the census flags it, and `V₀_def` needs the equation.

**(b) The ADR-124 blow-up** (`DECISIONS.md:9575-9591`: more than 3 GB at 14 s; quoted)
- The values do not depend on how symbols are stored (`"::"` = 1,569,048; `tcFnN 0` = 241,120,331,829; MEASURED). The cost comes from `triN`, so D7 neither causes nor cures it. It only removes the decode prefix, so the blow-up would arrive sooner.
- Turning the closed codes into variables works because the kernel evaluates Nat arithmetic eagerly only on terms with no free variables (INFERENCE; this is what `ev_tc_cons_der` at `:385-388` does). Keep doing that: never `simp`, `decide` or default `rfl` on closed hypotheses that contain symbol codes, `tcFnN`, TC0..TCONS, `V₀` or `nD`.
